ScamGuard SOS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

ScamGuard SOS helps you alert people you trust when you're in danger or being targeted by a scam. This page explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. In short: your contacts and personal details stay on your phone, we don't sell data, and we don't show ads.

Information that stays on your phone

  • Your emergency contacts (names and phone numbers)
  • Your name, your own note, your insurance policy number, and your blood type and allergies, if you add them
  • Your settings, such as country and nationality

These are saved in your browser's storage on your device. We don't receive them, and they are deleted if you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Your location

With your permission, the app reads your phone's location so it can include it in your alerts and show the right emergency numbers for the country you're in.

  • Country detection: your approximate coordinates are sent to BigDataCloud's reverse-geocoding service to find which country you're in. Only the coordinates are sent.
  • Live map: when you send an SOS, the app creates a private live-map link. While it's active, your location, accuracy, phone battery level and the name you entered are sent to our server so the people you alerted can follow you. The link works for 24 hours or until you tap "I'm safe" or "Stop sharing". The data is deleted within 7 days after the link ends.
  • The app only reads your location while it is open. It does not track you in the background.

Messages you send

SOS alerts are sent through your own SMS or WhatsApp app. You see each message before you press Send, and we never see their content.

If you chose your travel insurer in the app (this choice is saved only on your phone), or opened ScamGuard from an insurer's or partner's link, the medical, accident, evacuation and claims options let you message that partner on WhatsApp or call their assistance line. It includes the details you've entered (name, policy number, country, location and live-map link). After you send it, that message is handled by the partner under their own privacy policy.

Scam checker

Messages you paste into the scam checker are analysed on your phone. They are not uploaded or stored.

Analytics

  • Umami: counts page visits and button taps so we know which features are used. It doesn't use cookies or collect personal information.
  • Usage counts: our server keeps daily totals of how often the app is opened, installed, used to send an SOS, used to message a partner, or used to check a message, grouped by the link you opened it from. These are counts only. They contain no names, numbers, locations or message content, and can't identify you.
  • Hotjar: records anonymous usage, such as taps and scrolling, to help us improve the app's design. Fields with personal information (names, phone numbers, messages, location and the scam checker) are hidden from these recordings.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information.
  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't need an account, and we don't upload your address book.

Children

ScamGuard SOS is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices and contact

You can turn off location permission in your phone's settings at any time; alerts will still send, just without your location. To stop live sharing, tap "I'm safe" or "Stop sharing". To ask about your data or request deletion, see Delete your data or message us on WhatsApp at +65 8887 7041.
