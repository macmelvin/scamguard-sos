If you've been scammed
  1. Call your bank's 24-hour hotline right away to freeze your account and cards.
  2. Stop all contact with the scammer. Don't send any more money or codes.
  3. Report it to the police in the country where it happened and ask for a report number for your bank.
  4. Keep screenshots of chats, phone numbers and transaction records.
  5. Uninstall any app the caller asked you to install (e.g. AnyDesk, TeamViewer).

Numbers can change. Confirm local emergency numbers with your hotel or your country's embassy when you arrive.
