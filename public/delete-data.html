Data on your phone

Your emergency contacts, name, alert message, policy number and settings are stored only on your phone. To delete them:

  1. Open ScamGuard SOS, go to Contacts, scroll to Your data and tap Clear all data on this phone (tap twice to confirm). This also deletes your live-map data from our server. Or
  2. Uninstall the app, or go to Settings › Apps › ScamGuard SOS › Storage › Clear data.

This deletes them immediately. We never receive this data.

Data on our server

When you send an SOS, the live map stores your location, battery level and the name you entered. To delete it yourself, straight away:

  1. Open ScamGuard SOS on the phone that sent the SOS.
  2. Go to Contacts › Your data and tap Delete my live-map data (tap twice to confirm). The live-map link stops working and the locations are removed from our server immediately.

Only the phone that created a live map can delete it this way. If you've cleared the app since, or want analytics data removed, message us instead:

Request deletion on WhatsApp

What is deleted and what is kept

  • App install record (a random ID, setup and last-used dates, and country): deleted immediately when you tap Clear all data on this phone, and automatically after 13 months unused.
  • Live-map data (location, battery level, name): deleted on request. It is also deleted automatically within 7 days after the link ends, and links end after 24 hours at most.
  • Anonymous usage analytics (Umami page counts and Hotjar usage recordings, with personal fields hidden): deleted on request where it can be linked to you; otherwise removed under our analytics providers' retention periods, up to 365 days.
  • We don't keep any other data about you.
